# DailyNotch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 22, 2026_

DailyNotch is a focus timer and to-do list that lives in your MacBook's notch. It keeps everything on your Mac.

## What DailyNotch collects

Nothing. DailyNotch:

- has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking;
- does not send your tasks, notes, focus history or calendar events anywhere;
- does not share anything with third parties.

## Data stored on your Mac

Your tasks, notes, focus sessions and settings are saved in a single file inside the app's own storage on your Mac,
so they are there the next time you open it. They never leave your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

## Calendar

If you choose to connect your calendar, macOS asks for your permission first. DailyNotch then reads the events of the
day you are looking at to list them next to your tasks. It never creates, changes or deletes events, and it never
sends them anywhere. You can turn access off at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.

## Notifications

DailyNotch posts a local notification when a focus block ends, if you allow it. Notifications are created on your Mac;
nothing is sent to a server.

## Network

The Mac App Store version never connects to the internet. The version downloaded from GitHub asks GitHub, at most a
few times a day, whether a newer release exists; that request carries no personal data.
