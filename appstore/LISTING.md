## Review notes

DailyNotch runs from the menu bar (hourglass icon) and has no Dock icon until a window is open. Hover the mouse over
the top center of the screen (the notch, or a small pill on Macs without one) to open the dashboard. Menu bar >
Open Tasks shows the Tasks window; Menu bar > Settings shows the settings. Press Command-Shift-Space to start or stop a
focus block from any app. Calendar access is optional and read-only; the app works fully without it.

### Description (4000)

DailyNotch turns the space around your MacBook's notch into a quiet focus companion. Hover the notch to see today's
tasks and your activity, start a focus block with one click, and keep working while the countdown runs right next to
the camera, out of your way.

FOCUS IN THE NOTCH
• The countdown and the task you are working on sit on either side of the notch
• A progress line wraps around the notch and fills up as the block runs
• Minimal mode shows only the line, for a notch that barely changes
• Optional RGB line with a soft glow
• Command-Shift-Space starts or stops a block from any app

TASKS FOR TODAY
• Hover the notch for today's list: check off, start, reorder by dragging
• Each task has its own focus length, set with a tap
• Tasks window with a month calendar, unscheduled tasks, notes and quick add
• Days with open tasks are marked on the calendar

SEE YOUR STREAK
• A month of focus at a glance, in an activity grid
• Today's focus time, completed blocks and your current streak in Settings

CALENDAR, READ-ONLY
• See the day's events under your tasks
• DailyNotch only reads your calendar, never changes it

MADE FOR THE MAC
• Works on MacBooks with a notch, and as a small pill on any other screen
• Choose which display shows it
• A gentle chime and a notification when a block ends
• Open at login, English and Portuguese
• Settings window with search

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no network access. Your tasks stay on your Mac.
